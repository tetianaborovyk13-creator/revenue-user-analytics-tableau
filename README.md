## 📌 Executive Summary
This project delivers a comprehensive SaaS/Product analytics dashboard built in Tableau, supported by SQL data aggregation. The goal was to evaluate Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) dynamics, perform cohort and retention analysis, and segment users to drive data-informed business decisions.

### 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
* **LTV (Customer Lifetime Value):** $742.2
* **Paid Users:** 383
* **ARPPU (Average Revenue Per Paying User):** $164.9
* **Revenue Churn Rate:** 22.2%

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Methodology
* **SQL:** Data cleaning, aggregation, calculating user activity metrics, and preparing the relational dataset.
* **Tableau Public:** Advanced visualization, LOD Expressions (`FIXED`), Cohort/Retention Matrix heatmap, dual-axis charts, and interactive filtering.

---

## 📁 Repository Structure
* `Project SQL.sql` — SQL queries used for data extraction and metric preparation.
* `README.md` — Project overview, methodology, and key business insights.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
1. **High-Value Customer Segment:** Users aged **20–24** represent the primary revenue driver, demonstrating the highest ARPPU (> $210) combined with a low churn rate (~17%).
2. **Churn Risk Profile:** The **40–44** age bracket exhibits the highest Churn Rate (~78%) and lowest ARPPU, highlighting an area requiring targeted retention strategies or product onboarding adjustments.
3. **Cohort Retention:** Cohort analysis shows clear retention stabilization patterns over a 9-month customer lifetime frame.
